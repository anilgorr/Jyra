JYRA — ARCHITECTURE CONSOLIDATION V1
CONTROL PLANE + PROGRESSIVE IDENTITY TRUST + CAPABILITY-DRIVEN INTELLIGENCE

MODE:
ARCHITECTURE AUDIT → CONTROLLED REFACTOR → CROSS-DOMAIN VALIDATION

THIS IS NOT:
- another benchmark-specific fix
- a threshold tuning exercise
- a prompt tuning exercise
- a rewrite from scratch
- a multi-agent swarm project
- a final 50-company Reality Test
- an outreach/Explee project

PURPOSE:

JYRA has accumulated several individually correct systems, but repeated
validation has exposed a recurring architectural pattern:

1. information is missing
2. a downstream status becomes UNKNOWN / INSUFFICIENT
3. a safety gate stops execution
4. the resolution capability that could obtain the missing information
   is never invoked
5. the object remains UNKNOWN forever

Examples already observed:

UNKNOWN commercial role
→ buyer research blocked
→ company could not be understood

Then:

identity considered unsafe
→ company profile research blocked
→ evidence capable of strengthening identity was never acquired

The architecture must now distinguish:

RESOLVABLE UNCERTAINTY
from
GENUINE AMBIGUITY
from
UNSAFE CONDITIONS.

The core architectural principle is:

JYRA MUST ROUTE BASED ON
"WHAT INFORMATION IS MISSING AND WHAT ACTION IS SAFE NEXT?"

NOT merely:

"WHAT STATUS IS CURRENTLY STORED?"

==================================================
8. REASON-FIRST STATE MODEL
==================================================

Critical architecture change:

States such as:

UNKNOWN
INSUFFICIENT_DATA
BLOCKED

must no longer be actionable by themselves.

They require a machine-readable reason.

Examples:

UNKNOWN
reason = COMPANY_PROFILE_MISSING

UNKNOWN
reason = COMMERCIAL_RELATIONSHIP_AMBIGUOUS

INSUFFICIENT_DATA
reason = EMPLOYEE_SIZE_MISSING

BLOCKED
reason = IDENTITY_CONFLICT

These states have completely different next actions.

Introduce/consolidate stable reason codes.

Do not use free-text reason as workflow logic.

==================================================
9. THREE TYPES OF UNCERTAINTY
==================================================

Every unresolved prerequisite should conceptually fall into:

A. RESOLVABLE

Example:
primary business missing.

Action:
run Company Profile / Minimum Intelligence.

B. GENUINELY AMBIGUOUS

Example:
evidence exists, but commercial relationship cannot be determined.

Action:
remain UNKNOWN / monitor / human review if appropriate.

C. UNSAFE

Example:
two contradictory domains map to different companies.

Action:
stop attribution-sensitive operations.

The orchestrator must not treat A, B and C the same way.

==================================================
10. PROGRESSIVE IDENTITY TRUST
==================================================

Do NOT simply lower current identity thresholds.

Do NOT weaken wrong-entity protection.

Instead distinguish identity trust by ACTION RISK.

Current problem:

identity safety is effectively too binary.

JYRA needs conceptual separation between:

RESEARCH-SAFE IDENTITY

and

ATTRIBUTION-SAFE IDENTITY

and, later:

OUTREACH-SAFE IDENTITY.

A candidate may have enough identity confidence to:

- inspect its apparent official website
- collect public profile evidence
- build a provisional company profile
- strengthen identity resolution

without yet having enough certainty to:

- attach permanent canonical facts
- generate buyer signals
- rank the company as an opportunity
- enrich/contact people
- initiate outreach

11. IDENTITY PERMISSIONS
==================================================

Implement or derive action permissions from identity state.

Conceptually:

CAN_PUBLIC_PROFILE_RESEARCH
CAN_BUILD_PROVISIONAL_PROFILE

CAN_RUN_COMMERCIAL_ROLE
CAN_ATTACH_CANONICAL_FACTS
CAN_GENERATE_SIGNALS
CAN_RANK_OPPORTUNITY
CAN_ENRICH_CONTACTS

Do not necessarily create eight boolean DB columns.

Prefer a policy/resolver function based on:

identity state
confidence
evidence
conflict state
requested action.

Example:

CONFIRMED
→ all normal intelligence actions allowed.

PROBABLE with coherent domain evidence
→ public profile research allowed
→ provisional understanding allowed
→ permanent high-impact attribution only if current policy allows.

AMBIGUOUS with multiple conflicting entities
→ research may be restricted or blocked depending on resolvability.

WRONG_ENTITY
→ blocked.

NOT_A_COMPANY
→ blocked.
