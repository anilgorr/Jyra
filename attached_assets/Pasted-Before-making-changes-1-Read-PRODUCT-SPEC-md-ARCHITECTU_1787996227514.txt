SIGNAL DETECTION

Prefer deterministic rules.

Example:

If job facts show 2 security roles last period and 9 now:
possible SECURITY_HIRING_ACCELERATION.

AI may assist only where classification is ambiguous.

Example:

Does "VP Trust and Safety" count as senior cybersecurity leadership?

SIGNAL DECAY

Signals weaken over time.

Store lifetime/decay configuration.

Current strength should be recalculated from:

original strength
effective date
lifetime
decay rule

Do not delete expired signals.

Mark them inactive/stale.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

Facts must generate signals deterministically where rules exist.

No signal may exist without supporting fact/evidence.

Old signals must decay.

Same company can have different project-specific signals.
