5. IMPORTANT INPUT RULE
==================================================

This Bright Data dataset requires a LinkedIn company URL.

Therefore Bright Data MUST NOT assume:

company name
domain
website URL

can be sent directly to this dataset.

Preferred input:

verified LinkedIn company URL

Also allow:

probable LinkedIn company URL

but record entity confidence.

If LinkedIn company URL is missing:

return:

IDENTIFIER_NOT_SUPPORTED

or equivalent structured result.

Do NOT fabricate a LinkedIn URL.

Do NOT automatically search LinkedIn in this integration milestone.

LinkedIn URL resolution can be added separately later.

==================================================
11. ENTITY MATCHING
==================================================

Before attaching returned data to canonical company:

compare:

requested LinkedIn URL
returned LinkedIn URL
company name
website/domain
country/location
other identity information

Classify:

CONFIRMED
PROBABLE
AMBIGUOUS
WRONG

Rules:

CONFIRMED:
strong LinkedIn URL match and identity consistent

PROBABLE:
strong identity but one identifier differs or is missing

AMBIGUOUS:
multiple plausible companies or weak identity

WRONG:
returned company clearly differs

Only CONFIRMED and approved PROBABLE results may update canonical attributes.

AMBIGUOUS must:

not overwrite canonical attributes
be stored for review

WRONG must:

not attach to canonical company
