---
name: ICP evidence gaps
description: Defines how WHO represents missing fit evidence and when company firmographics are worth requesting.
---

WHO must distinguish missing evidence from negative fit. Only configured ICP dimensions may be reported as missing, and the result must identify each absent dimension with a stable code. Multiple gaps need both the exact dimensions and an aggregate multiple-dimensions code.

**Why:** A broad insufficient-data result cannot tell the control plane whether another provider call can change the decision, while treating absent values as failures incorrectly turns unresearched companies into negative-fit conclusions.

**How to apply:** Recommend company firmographics only when WHO is insufficient because geography, industry, or employee-size evidence required by the active ICP is absent. Do not recommend it for unconfigured dimensions or unknowns that firmographics cannot resolve.
