---
name: Timeless fact dates
description: Date semantics for extracting stable company facts versus dated events.
---

Stable present-tense company facts may use the evidence observation date as their effective date. Event facts must continue to use a date explicitly supported by the source.

**Why:** Requiring every fact to have a source-stated event date made legitimate service, technology, location, and capability statements in undated company pages impossible to extract.

**How to apply:** Use the evidence observation date only for timeless statements. Never use it to manufacture an appointment, funding, hiring, incident, expansion, or other event date.
