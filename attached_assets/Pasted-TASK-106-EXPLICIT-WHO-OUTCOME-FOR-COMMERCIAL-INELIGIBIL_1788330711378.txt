5. CORE SEMANTIC PRINCIPLE
==================================================

JYRA must distinguish:

UNKNOWN BECAUSE WE CANNOT DECIDE

from

KNOWN NOT TO BE A BUYER FOR THIS OFFERING

These are fundamentally different.

Example:

CommercialRole = UNKNOWN

means:

The seller-relative commercial relationship could not be established.

WHO may therefore remain unresolved / INSUFFICIENT_DATA depending on existing policy.

But:

CommercialRole = SELLER_COMPETITOR

means:

The company has already been conclusively classified as selling an offering that materially competes with the seller's specific offering.

For normal buyer-targeting purposes, this is sufficient to deterministically resolve:

WHO = LIKELY_NOT_FIT

This is not a new LLM inference.

It is a deterministic buyer-eligibility policy based on an already resolved seller-relative Commercial Role.

7. DO NOT COLLAPSE COMMERCIAL ROLE AND WHO
==================================================

Commercial Role and WHO remain separate concepts.

Commercial Role asks:

"Can this company reasonably buy, compete with, or partner around this specific seller offering?"

WHO asks:

"Does this company fit this seller's current buyer ICP?"

DO NOT implement:

POTENTIAL_BUYER → LIKELY_FIT

A POTENTIAL_BUYER can still be:

LIKELY_FIT
POSSIBLE_FIT

INSUFFICIENT_DATA

depending on ICP.

Do NOT automatically convert:

ADJACENT_VENDOR → LIKELY_NOT_FIT

or

PARTNER_POSSIBLE → LIKELY_NOT_FIT

unless an existing generic policy already explicitly requires it.

For Task #106, the required deterministic exclusion is only:

→ LIKELY_NOT_FIT
