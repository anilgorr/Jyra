==================================================
13. BUYER ROLE DECISION PRINCIPLE
==================================================

Conceptually reason in this order:

1. Is this the correct real company?

2. What does the candidate company primarily do?

3. What exactly does the seller offer?

4. Does the candidate substantially sell the same offering?

   YES:
   SELLER_COMPETITOR

5. Does the candidate sell a complementary/adjacent offering?

   YES:
   ADJACENT_VENDOR
   unless strong existing semantics justify PARTNER_POSSIBLE.

6. Is this type of organization structurally capable of consuming the
   seller's offering?

7. Is there sufficient supported profile information to classify it?

   YES:
   POTENTIAL_BUYER where appropriate.

   NO:
   UNKNOWN.

Do not use ICP fit itself as proof of buyer role.

==================================================
14. IMPORTANT SEPARATION
==================================================

Keep these concepts separate:

Do not combine them into one classifier.

      ↓

      ↓
