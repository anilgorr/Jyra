==================================================
4. DISCOVERY VS RESEARCH
==================================================

Keep these concepts separate.

COMPANY_DISCOVERY answers:

"What companies may belong to this market?"

WEB_SEARCH answers:

"What current public evidence exists about this known company?"

WEBSITE_CRAWL answers:

"What does this known company's official website say?"

Do not perform deep research on every discovered company.

==================================================

==================================================

This is deliberately small.

Do not discover hundreds or thousands yet.

7. ENTITY RESOLUTION
==================================================

Run candidates through canonical company resolution.

Normalize:

company name
domain
www/non-www
http/https
LinkedIn company URL

Detect duplicates against:

existing imported companies
existing discovered companies
current project companies

Do not create a second company simply because the same company came from
another provider/query.

==================================================
8. MISSING DOMAIN
==================================================

Domain is NOT mandatory for discovery ingestion.

If a candidate lacks a domain:

attempt cheap domain resolution using existing supported mechanisms.

Use:

company name
LinkedIn URL
geography
industry
description
public evidence

Generate candidate domains.

Auto-accept only when confidence is sufficiently high under existing
governance rules.

Ambiguous:
NEEDS REVIEW

Unresolved:
UNKNOWN

Never guess a domain.

==================================================
9. CHEAP QUALIFICATION
==================================================

Do NOT immediately run Tavily + Apify for all 20 companies.

First evaluate cheap available attributes against ICP.

Classify candidates using existing architecture into something like:

LIKELY_FIT
POSSIBLE_FIT
INSUFFICIENT_DATA
LIKELY_NOT_FIT

Do not introduce these exact enums if an equivalent governed model
already exists.

Missing data must not become a disqualifier.

==================================================
10. RESEARCH PRIORITIZATION
==================================================

After cheap qualification, determine which candidates deserve additional
research.

Research Planner should consider:

cache

Do not actually perform deep research yet unless required by existing
workflow.

For this test, preferably STOP after producing the prioritized research
queue.

==================================================
11. SIGNALS ARE NOT DISCOVERY FILTERS
==================================================

Do not require companies to already have:

security hiring
security leadership changes
funding
security-stack changes

to enter the candidate universe.

Those are opportunity/timing signals discovered later.

ICP determines whether the company is worth considering.

Signals determine whether NOW may be a good time.
