---
name: Progressive identity permissions
description: Action-risk boundary between provisional public research and permanent company attribution.
---

Identity trust is permission-specific, not one universal boolean. A coherent probable discovery identity with an exact domain and no conflict may perform bounded public profile research and project-scoped provisional semantic understanding. Canonical facts, signals, opportunity ranking, contacts, and outreach require attribution-safe identity.

**Why:** A binary attribution-safe prerequisite created a deadlock: candidates lacking profile evidence were blocked from the safe resolver capable of obtaining that evidence. Simply lowering the canonical trust threshold would instead risk wrong-entity contamination.

**How to apply:** Derive permissions from the newest identity state, exact identifier evidence, and conflicts. Preserve provisional evidence under a distinct project-scoped provenance type; promote or attach only after independent verification. Ambiguous, wrong-entity, not-a-company, and conflicting states remain fail-closed.
