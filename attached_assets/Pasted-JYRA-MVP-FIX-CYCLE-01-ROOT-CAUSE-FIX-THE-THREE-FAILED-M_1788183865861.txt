EXTRACTION PRINCIPLE
==================================================

Fact extraction must extract OBSERVATIONS.

Example:

SOURCE:

"Acme appointed Jane Smith as Chief Information Security Officer on
August 12, 2026."

GOOD FACT:

type = PERSON_ROLE_CHANGE
person = Jane Smith
role = Chief Information Security Officer
company = Acme
event_date = 2026-08-12

BAD FACT:

"Acme has increased cybersecurity intent."

==================================================
SIZE LOGIC
==================================================

ICP:

100–2,000 employees
sweet spot 200–1,000.

Do not treat overlapping ranges as exact matches.

Examples:

1,001–5,000

overlaps target but cannot prove company <=2,000.

Therefore classification must preserve uncertainty.

Likewise:

51–200

partially overlaps lower boundary.

Use:

MATCH
PARTIAL_MATCH
NO_MATCH
UNKNOWN

at dimension level where appropriate.

Do not convert PARTIAL_MATCH automatically to full fit.

==================================================

==================================================

HEADQUARTERS_COUNTRY

an employee or office in a target country unless the approved ICP
