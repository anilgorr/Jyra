---
name: Buyer-market discovery intent
description: Why market discovery must start from the buyer ICP rather than the seller offering.
---

Discovery queries must be led by the buyer ICP's company types, industries,
geographies, size, and exclusions. The seller offering/category belongs in
negative relationship classification, not in the positive search concept.

**Why:** Offering-centered queries can retrieve companies that sell the same
offering, producing a high-quality list of competitors while appearing relevant
to semantic search.

**How to apply:** Build several bounded buyer-market slices from approved ICP
constraints. Classify seller/competitor and adjacent-vendor relationships before
WHO, and preserve UNKNOWN when candidate evidence is missing.
