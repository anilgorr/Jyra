PHASE 12 — DYNAMIC OPPORTUNITY INTELLIGENCE PACK BUILDER

JYRA
Who. When. Why.

IMPORTANT CONTEXT:

Phases 0–11 and Phase 11A have already been implemented.

JYRA is a GENERIC B2B Opportunity Intelligence platform.

DO NOT hardcode cybersecurity, ISO 27001, recruitment, marketing, SaaS,
solar, IT services or any other industry into the core architecture.

The purpose of this phase is to allow JYRA to understand:

"What should I look for in the market that may indicate a company needs
what THIS customer sells?"

==================================================
CORE CONCEPT
==================================================

Every seller is different.

Therefore JYRA should dynamically create an:

OPPORTUNITY INTELLIGENCE PACK

based on:

Business Twin
+
ICP
+
Offering
+
Customer-provided assumptions
+
existing approved signal definitions

The Intelligence Pack defines what JYRA should research and monitor.

==================================================
1. INTELLIGENCE PACK
==================================================

Create appropriate schema such as:

intelligence_packs
intelligence_pack_versions
intelligence_pack_signals
intelligence_pack_research_questions

Reuse existing Signal Pack structures if appropriate.

Do NOT create redundant tables if existing architecture supports this cleanly.

Each pack belongs to:

organization
project
offering/business twin version
ICP version

Support versioning.

==================================================
2. GENERATION WORKFLOW
==================================================

After Business Twin + ICP exist:

JYRA should be able to propose an Intelligence Pack.

Use:

Business Twin
ICP
offering
problems solved
buyer roles
urgency triggers
differentiators
negative criteria
known customer patterns if available

Ask:

"What changes or events usually cause a company to need what you sell?"

Allow customer input.

Then generate candidate signals.

==================================================
3. AI ROLE
==================================================

AI may PROPOSE signal definitions.

AI must NOT silently activate them.

Workflow:

AI proposes
→
JYRA validates structure
→
Customer reviews
→
Customer approves/edits/removes/adds
→
Signal becomes ACTIVE

Use strict structured output.

==================================================
4. SIGNAL PROPOSAL
==================================================

For each proposed signal show:

Signal Name
Description
Why It Matters
Category
Need Impact
Timing Impact
Likely Evidence
Where JYRA Could Detect It
Expected Lifetime
Suggested Strength
Potential False Positives
Confidence Requirements

Do not claim the signal is predictive before outcome evidence exists.

Label initial signals appropriately as:

HYPOTHESIS

==================================================
5. SIGNAL TYPES
==================================================

Allow packs to contain:

POSITIVE SIGNALS
TIMING SIGNALS
NEED SIGNALS
NEGATIVE SIGNALS
DISQUALIFYING CONDITIONS
MONITORING SIGNALS

Do not assume every signal directly means buying intent.

==================================================
6. RESEARCH QUESTIONS
==================================================

For every approved signal, derive research questions.

Example generic structure:

Signal:
Leadership change

Research questions:
- Has a relevant executive joined recently?
- When?
- What responsibility does that executive have?
- Is the role relevant to the seller's offering?

Another seller may not care about this signal.

Research questions must be contextual.

==================================================
7. SOURCE STRATEGY
==================================================

For each research question define potential source capabilities:

WEBSITE_CRAWL
WEB_SEARCH
JOB_SEARCH
NEWS_SEARCH
TECH_STACK
LEADERSHIP_SEARCH
PUBLIC_SOCIAL_SEARCH

Do not bind questions permanently to one provider.

Use capabilities.

Provider Router decides provider later.

==================================================
8. CUSTOMER EDITING
==================================================

Customer can:

Approve
Edit
Disable
Delete
Add Custom Signal
Change Importance
Change Signal Lifetime
Change Need Impact
Change Timing Impact
Add Research Question
Remove Research Question

Version changes.

==================================================
9. STARTUP HANDLING
==================================================

If Business Twin is based mainly on founder hypotheses:

mark Intelligence Pack:

HYPOTHESIS-LED

Explain:

"These signals are based primarily on your current assumptions.
JYRA will help evaluate them as outcomes accumulate."

For established businesses with strong historical data:

mark appropriately:

EVIDENCE-INFORMED

Do not pretend historical correlation exists unless actual data supports it.

"understanding what THIS seller sells and determining what market changes
could indicate opportunity for THIS seller."
