==================================================

"Who could plausibly BUY what this Business Twin sells?"

"Which companies are semantically similar to what this Business Twin sells?"

not recruitment agencies.

Solar installer

must discover facility owners / energy buyers,

not solar companies.

ERP vendor

must discover businesses that may need ERP,

not ERP vendors.

This buyer/seller distinction must be generic.
