5. HUMAN REVIEW AUTHORITY
==================================================

For factual company fields:

public evidence is authoritative.

Reviewer must not override clear evidence merely based on intuition.

For CommercialRole and WHO:

the seller-relative commercial definition is authoritative.

A knowledgeable seller/domain owner may legitimately determine:

"Yes, I would consider this type of organization a plausible customer"

even if the company's website never says it needs the offering.

Do NOT confuse:

POTENTIAL_BUYER

with:

CURRENT PURCHASE INTENT.

Potential Buyer means structurally plausible customer.

WHEN/WHY determines whether there is current opportunity evidence.

==================================================
6. IMPORTANT ROLE SEMANTICS
==================================================

POTENTIAL_BUYER

Company could reasonably consume the seller's offering and is not
primarily selling a substantially overlapping offering.

SELLER_COMPETITOR

Company materially offers substantially the same service to similar
customers.

ADJACENT_VENDOR

Company sells a related/complementary offering but is not the direct
customer relationship being targeted.

PARTNER_POSSIBLE

Commercially plausible partnership/referral/integration/reseller
relationship is more natural than direct buyer relationship.

UNKNOWN

Evidence or commercial relationship genuinely insufficient/ambiguous.

Do NOT use industry alone.

Do NOT infer intent.
