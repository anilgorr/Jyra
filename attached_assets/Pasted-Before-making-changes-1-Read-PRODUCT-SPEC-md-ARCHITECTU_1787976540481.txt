PURPOSE

The ICP converts the Business Twin into objective company-selection rules.

CRITERION TYPES

MUST_HAVE
PREFERRED
DISQUALIFIER
ADVISORY

MUST_HAVE

Mandatory objective condition.

Example:

Country IN India, UAE

PREFERRED

Improves Fit but is not mandatory.

Preferred criteria may have configurable weights.

DISQUALIFIER

Confirmed condition makes the account unsuitable.

ADVISORY

Commercially meaningful observation that cannot yet be reliably evaluated from structured data.
