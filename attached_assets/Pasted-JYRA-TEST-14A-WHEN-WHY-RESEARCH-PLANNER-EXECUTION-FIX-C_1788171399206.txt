2. REQUIRED PLANNER SEMANTICS
==================================================

Research questions are independent unless evidence from one question
actually answers another.

For Managed SOC, these four areas are independent:

SECURITY_LEADERSHIP
SECURITY_HIRING
FUNDED_RISK_PROGRAM
SECURITY_STACK_CHANGE

Failure to find evidence for SECURITY_LEADERSHIP must NOT cause:

SECURITY_HIRING
FUNDED_RISK_PROGRAM
SECURITY_STACK_CHANGE

to be deferred automatically.

Likewise, finding evidence for one category must not automatically
satisfy another category.

Use INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE unless credible evidence actually supports a
negative conclusion.

==================================================
4. QUESTION-LEVEL STOP CONDITIONS
==================================================

Stop researching an individual question when:

A. sufficient credible evidence answers it,

OR

B. reasonable attempts find no useful evidence,

OR

C. additional research has genuinely low expected information value,

OR

D. budget is exhausted.

Stopping QUESTION A must not stop Questions B/C/D unless there is an
explicit documented reason.

==================================================
5. COMPANY-LEVEL STOP CONDITIONS
==================================================

Do not stop research for a company merely because its first question
produces no signal.

Company-level early stopping is allowed only when:

a hard disqualifier appears,

OR

the company exceeds research budget,

OR

sufficient evidence already answers the opportunity hypothesis across
the required dimensions,

OR

remaining questions are explicitly proven redundant.

Record the stop reason.
