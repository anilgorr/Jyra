==================================================
4. SIGNAL MEANING IS CONTEXTUAL
==================================================

The same fact can mean different things for different sellers.

Example:

FACT:
Company raised $20M.

For recruitment company:
May indicate hiring/growth opportunity.

For ERP consultancy:
May indicate scaling/infrastructure opportunity.

For ISO consultant:
Funding alone may mean almost nothing.

Therefore:

FACTS may be reusable.

SIGNAL INTERPRETATION must be contextual to:

Business Twin
+
ICP
+
Offering
+
Signal Pack
+
Project

Do not globally assign universal buying meaning to facts.

==================================================
6. SIGNAL PACKS
==================================================

A Signal Pack represents:

"What market events and conditions may indicate opportunity for THIS offering?"

Signal Packs must be project/offering aware.

A customer may eventually have:

Company A
Offering 1
Signal Pack 1

Company A
Offering 2
Signal Pack 2

The same target company may therefore produce different opportunity
interpretations for different offerings.

==================================================
7. SIGNAL PROVENANCE
==================================================

Every generated signal must preserve:

project
company
signal definition
supporting facts
supporting evidence
generation method
created timestamp
observed timestamp
confidence
strength
decay
model/version

A signal without supporting evidence/facts must not be treated as confirmed.

==================================================
8. DO NOT LET AI INVENT SIGNALS DURING ANALYSIS
==================================================

Later AI may propose Signal Definitions.

But during production opportunity analysis:

AI must NOT spontaneously invent a new signal and use it for scoring.

Only active/approved Signal Definitions may influence opportunity scoring.
