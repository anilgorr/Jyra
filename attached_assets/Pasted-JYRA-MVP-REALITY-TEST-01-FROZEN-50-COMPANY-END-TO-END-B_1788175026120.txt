JYRA — MVP REALITY TEST 01
FROZEN 50-COMPANY END-TO-END BENCHMARK

PURPOSE

This is the first full product-level validation of JYRA.

Do NOT treat this as another component test.

We want to answer one question:

"If a real customer gives JYRA their business/offering, can JYRA find
the right companies, identify which ones deserve attention now, explain
why with evidence, identify who should be approached, and produce a
usable sales-priority list at economically viable cost?"

Run the complete existing JYRA MVP pipeline.
