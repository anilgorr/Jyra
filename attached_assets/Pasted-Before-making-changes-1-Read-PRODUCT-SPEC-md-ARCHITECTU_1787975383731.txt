Build DigiSignal Business Twin.

PURPOSE

The Business Twin is DigiSignal's structured understanding of the seller.

It answers:

What does this company sell?
Which problems does it solve?
Which companies receive the most value?
Who normally buys?
What triggers urgency?
Why do strong customers buy?
Which customers are poor fits?
What makes the seller different?

DATABASE

Create:

business_twins
business_twin_versions

Never overwrite original user answers.

Every regeneration or meaningful interpretation change creates a new version.
