==================================================
14. COMMERCIAL ROLE SEMANTICS
==================================================

POTENTIAL_BUYER:

The candidate primarily operates a business that could reasonably consume
the seller's offering and does not materially sell the same offering.

SELLER_COMPETITOR:

The candidate materially sells substantially the same offering/capability to
customers.

ADJACENT_VENDOR:

The candidate sells a related/complementary product or service but is not
primarily a normal end buyer for this offering.

PARTNER_POSSIBLE:

Use only where evidence strongly supports a plausible partner/channel/
complementary commercial relationship.

Do NOT force partner detection for MVP.

UNKNOWN:

Evidence is insufficient or relationship is genuinely ambiguous.
