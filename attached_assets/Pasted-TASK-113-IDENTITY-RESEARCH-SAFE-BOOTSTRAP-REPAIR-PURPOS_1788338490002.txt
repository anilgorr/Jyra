This creates a circular dependency:

identity evidence insufficient
→ identity UNSAFE
→ research forbidden
→ identity evidence can never be gathered

This is inconsistent with JYRA's progressive identity-trust architecture.

The required architecture is:

candidate identity
→ RESEARCH_SAFE

→ identity corroboration
→ ATTRIBUTION_SAFE
→ canonical facts
→ Company Understanding
→ CommercialRole
→ WHO

Task #113 must repair ONLY this generic identity/research permission boundary.

==================================================
3. PRESERVE THE THREE TRUST LEVELS
==================================================

The system must preserve distinct identity permissions:

UNSAFE
RESEARCH_SAFE
ATTRIBUTION_SAFE

Do NOT collapse these levels.

Meaning:

UNSAFE

The candidate is too ambiguous or contradictory even for entity-directed research.

Examples:

- malformed/invalid domain
- unresolved same-name entity with no distinguishing identifier
- strong conflicting identity evidence
- candidate domain clearly belongs to another organization
- unresolved entity collision
- unsafe cross-company attribution risk

--------------------------------------------------

RESEARCH_SAFE

JYRA has enough identifying information to inspect public sources ABOUT the candidate, but not enough evidence yet to attach factual claims canonically.

Research performed while RESEARCH_SAFE must enter the provisional evidence workspace.

RESEARCH_SAFE must NOT permit:

- canonical fact attribution
- signals
- opportunity decisions
- outreach/contact attribution
- automatic canonical entity merging

--------------------------------------------------

ATTRIBUTION_SAFE

Independent evidence sufficiently corroborates the candidate identity.

Only then may evidence/facts be promoted from provisional workspace into the canonical intelligence store according to existing evidence-admission rules.

==================================================
4. GENERIC RESEARCH-SAFE BOOTSTRAP RULE
==================================================

Implement a deterministic generic bootstrap policy.

A candidate MAY become RESEARCH_SAFE when all of the following are true:

A. a syntactically valid normalized domain is available

B. the domain is explicitly associated with the candidate through a legitimate source, such as:

- user/imported company row
- company discovery provider result
- existing company record
- explicit company URL
- deterministic entity-resolution output

C. there is no known contradictory identity evidence

D. there is no unresolved collision indicating the domain may belong to a different company

E. the candidate is sufficiently identifiable for targeted public research

IMPORTANT:

Exact domain alone is NOT enough for ATTRIBUTION_SAFE.

It is only enough, with valid provenance and no contradiction, to permit bounded RESEARCH_SAFE public investigation.
