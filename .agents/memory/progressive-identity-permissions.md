---
name: Progressive identity permissions
description: Action-risk boundary between provisional public research and permanent company attribution.
---

Identity trust is permission-specific, not one universal boolean. A coherent domain-associated identity with no known conflict may perform bounded public profile research and store project/company-scoped provisional evidence. Company Understanding, CommercialRole, canonical facts, signals, opportunity ranking, contacts, and outreach require attribution-safe identity. Attribution safety requires an independent second identity association; one provider assertion, including confirmed discovery, cannot promote itself.

**Why:** A binary attribution-safe prerequisite created a deadlock: candidates lacking profile evidence were blocked from the safe resolver capable of obtaining that evidence. A blind holdout later reproduced this at the ordinary import boundary: all exact-domain identities resolved, but none could run bounded research. Simply lowering the canonical trust threshold would instead risk wrong-entity contamination.

**How to apply:** Validate and normalize the domain, require legitimate intake/discovery/canonical-record association for research, and scan all known identity history for contradiction or unresolved collision. Preserve bootstrap evidence as private project/company-scoped review provenance. Reassess deterministically before canonical attachment, and require a separate verified association for promotion. Never run attribution-sensitive enrichment while the identity is only research-safe.
