PHASE 14 — GENERIC OPPORTUNITY ENGINE

JYRA
Who. When. Why.

==================================================
OBJECTIVE
==================================================

Transform evidence-backed company intelligence into a transparent,
deterministic Opportunity assessment.

JYRA must evaluate:

FIT
NEED
TIMING
RELATIONSHIP

and separately:

CONFIDENCE

==================================================
1. DATA MODEL
==================================================

Create/extend:

opportunities
opportunity_history
opportunity_score_components
model_versions

Preserve historical state.

==================================================
2. DIMENSIONS
==================================================

FIT:
How closely does the company match the ICP?

NEED:
How much evidence suggests the company may have the problem JYRA's customer solves?

TIMING:
How much evidence suggests the problem may matter now?

RELATIONSHIP:
What existing relationship does the customer have with the account?

CONFIDENCE:
How confident is JYRA in the underlying information?

==================================================
3. INITIAL WEIGHTS
==================================================

Default:

Fit = 30%
Need = 30%
Timing = 30%
Relationship = 10%

Make configurable/versioned.

Confidence is NOT part of the opportunity score.

High confidence should never artificially make a weak opportunity strong.

==================================================
4. MISSING DATA
==================================================

Missing information does NOT mean failure.

Unknown ≠ negative.

Example:

employee count unknown

must not automatically mean:

ICP mismatch.

Represent uncertainty explicitly.

==================================================
5. RELATIONSHIP
==================================================

Use first-party data when available.

Examples:

existing customer
past customer
open opportunity
lost opportunity
previous contact
meeting held
known champion
no relationship

Do not fabricate relationship data.

==================================================
6. CONFIDENCE
==================================================

Calculate separately using factors such as:

source authority
freshness
directness
corroboration
independent source count
contradictions
information completeness

Keep deterministic and explainable.

==================================================
7. OPPORTUNITY STATES
==================================================

Implement:

DORMANT
WATCH
EMERGING
RISING
SURGING
ACTIVE
COOLING

State depends on:

Fit
Need
Timing
Relationship where relevant
signal strength
clusters
confidence gates
movement over time

Use versioned deterministic rules.

==================================================
8. GATING
==================================================

Prevent false positives.

Examples:

High Fit + no Need evidence
must not automatically become Surging.

Strong Need + poor Fit
must not automatically become Surging.

Weak Confidence
may require:

NEEDS_MORE_RESEARCH

rather than a strong conclusion.

==================================================
9. DECAY
==================================================

Signals age.

Opportunity strength should change as evidence becomes stale.

Persist state transitions.

==================================================
10. PROJECT-SPECIFIC INTERPRETATION
==================================================

The SAME company can have:

SURGING

for Seller A

and:

DORMANT

for Seller B.

This is expected.

Never create a universal company buying-intent score.

==================================================
11. EXPLAINABILITY
==================================================

For every component preserve:

score
rule
signals
clusters
facts
evidence
model version

JYRA must be able to answer:

"Why did you rank this company?"

==================================================
12. MODEL VERSIONING
==================================================

Never silently change scoring behavior.

Example:

Opportunity Model v1.0
Opportunity Model v1.1

Store which version produced each historical assessment.

==================================================
ACCEPTANCE
==================================================

Test:

high Fit/no Need
high Need/low Fit
high Timing/weak Confidence
strong cluster
negative signals
stale signals
contradictory evidence
missing data
customer relationship data
same company/different sellers

Update OPPORTUNITY_ENGINE.md.
Update CHANGELOG.md.
Create checkpoint.

DO NOT begin Phase 15.
