4. GENERIC PRINCIPLE
==================================================

The corrected generic rule should be:

Semantic assessment SHOULD proceed when:

1. Identity is safe enough for the requested action
AND
2. Minimum Company Intelligence is sufficient
AND
3. There is enough evidence to understand the candidate's primary commercial activity

Primary commercial activity may be established from one or more reliable evidence-backed fields such as:

- primary business description
- company description
- products/services
- homepage/service-page description
- business model
- industry plus descriptive evidence
- other existing evidence-backed company profile text

Do NOT require every company profile field to be populated.

In particular:

Missing or sparse:

- products/services
- employee size
- technology
- geography
- compliance
- secondary business details

must NOT automatically make Company Understanding semantically impossible if primary commercial activity can already be understood.

Missing data should remain UNKNOWN downstream where appropriate.

Missing information ≠ negative evidence.
