SIMPLE EVIDENCE-FIRST PIPELINE

7/16 = 43.8%

WHO coverage:

The system is conservative and relatively safe, but the execution architecture is too fragmented.

The current chain contains too many independent gates:

identity permission
→ provisional research
→ attribution promotion
→ MCI
→ readiness
→ Company Understanding
→ CommercialRole
→ evidence handoff
→ WHO
→ multiple policy layers

The new architecture must simplify this to:

DISCOVER
→ RESEARCH
→ BUILD COMPANY PROFILE
→ DECIDE COMMERCIAL ROLE + WHO
→ APPLY SMALL DETERMINISTIC SAFETY LAYER
→ OUTPUT

This is:

JYRA INTELLIGENCE CORE V2

The objective is NOT to preserve V1 architecture.

The objective is to create the simplest reliable evidence-backed intelligence pipeline capable of answering:

WHO should I target?

and later enabling:

WHEN?
WHY?

4. STAGE 1 — SIMPLE COMPANY RESOLUTION
==================================================

Input:

domain

businessId
offeringId
projectId

company name

URL

Identity logic must be simple.

If:

A. normalized domain is syntactically valid

AND

B. domain came from a legitimate source such as:

- CSV/import
- company discovery
- user entry
- existing company record
- provider result

AND

C. first-party website responds

AND

D. website reasonably identifies the expected company/brand

AND

E. no contradictory evidence exists

THEN:

identityStatus =
RESOLVED

Do NOT require multiple independent corroborating providers for an ordinary exact-domain company.

Do NOT create an identity deadlock.

--------------------------------------------------

Use:

IDENTITY_UNCERTAIN

only when there is a REAL ambiguity, such as:

- domain belongs to another company
- same-name collision
- parked domain
- inaccessible website plus no reliable corroboration
- conflicting organization identity
- acquisition/rebrand ambiguity
- unresolved subsidiary/parent mismatch

--------------------------------------------------

Important:

Lack of employee count
lack of HQ
lack of LinkedIn data
lack of technology data

DO NOT make identity uncertain.

Identity answers only:

"Are we researching the correct company?"

==================================================

7. What other offices/geographies are supported?

Research only what is required to make:

and

decisions.

16. COMMERCIAL ROLE SEMANTICS
==================================================

Allowed:

POTENTIAL_BUYER
SELLER_COMPETITOR
ADJACENT_VENDOR
PARTNER_POSSIBLE
UNKNOWN

Decision order:

FIRST:
Does the candidate materially sell the same outcome/capability as the seller's specific offering?

If YES:
SELLER_COMPETITOR

Competition requires material substitutability.

Same industry does NOT equal competitor.

--------------------------------------------------

SECOND:
Is there affirmative evidence of a genuinely complementary commercial/vendor relationship?

If YES:
ADJACENT_VENDOR or PARTNER_POSSIBLE

Do not use ADJACENT_VENDOR merely because both companies sell B2B services.

--------------------------------------------------

THIRD:
Can the company structurally consume/buy the seller's offering?

If YES and no stronger relationship applies:
POTENTIAL_BUYER

--------------------------------------------------

Otherwise:
UNKNOWN

==================================================
17. CRITICAL COMMERCIAL ROLE RULES
==================================================

These must be explicit in the assessment instructions.

A marketing agency may still buy specialist marketing capability.

A cybersecurity company may still buy security services.

An IT services company may still buy managed services.

A SaaS company selling adjacent software may still be a buyer.

Do not classify based only on shared vocabulary.

Material offering substitutability matters.

==================================================
18. WHO SEMANTICS
==================================================

Allowed:

LIKELY_FIT
POSSIBLE_FIT
LIKELY_NOT_FIT
INSUFFICIENT_DATA

WHO answers:

"Is this structurally a company the seller should target?"

WHO is NOT:

"Will they buy right now?"

Do NOT require:

funding signal
hiring signal
active compliance project
recent executive change
active buying trigger

Those belong to WHEN/WHY.

==================================================
19. WHO DECISION PRINCIPLES
==================================================

LIKELY_FIT

Strong evidence supports all meaningful mandatory ICP criteria and no exclusion applies.

--------------------------------------------------

POSSIBLE_FIT

Structurally plausible buyer, but:

- one important criterion is uncertain
OR
- fit is plausible but not strong enough for LIKELY_FIT

--------------------------------------------------

LIKELY_NOT_FIT

A mandatory ICP condition clearly fails

OR

an explicit exclusion applies

OR

resolved CommercialRole = SELLER_COMPETITOR

--------------------------------------------------

INSUFFICIENT_DATA

Evidence is genuinely insufficient to determine structural fit.

Do not use INSUFFICIENT_DATA merely because optional information is missing.

21. EVIDENCE RULE
==================================================

Every meaningful factual proposition in:

must map to evidence IDs.
