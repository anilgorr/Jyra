LLM EXTRACTION

AI is untrusted.

SUPPORT

The supporting excerpt must appear in or be directly derivable from the stored source content.

Reject unsupported claims.

IMPORTANT SEPARATION

Source:

"We are hiring an Information Security Manager responsible for maintaining our ISO 27001 ISMS."

Valid facts:

Company is hiring an Information Security Manager.

Job responsibility mentions ISO 27001.

Job responsibility mentions ISMS.

Invalid fact:

Company needs an external ISO 27001 consultant.

That is an interpretation, not a fact.

Do not store:

high buying intent
likely to purchase
needs our solution
good opportunity

as facts.
