Build DigiSignal's Research Planner.

CORE PRODUCT REQUIREMENT

DigiSignal must decide what it needs to learn about each company.

It must not blindly query every source for every account.

The Planner should ask:

What do we already know?

What do we not know?

Which missing information could materially change:

Fit?
Need?
Timing?
Confidence?

What is the cheapest useful way to answer it?

STAGED RESEARCH

STAGE 1 — CHEAP QUALIFICATION

Use:

existing global company cache
domain
country
industry
employee size
basic website information

Purpose:

determine basic ICP plausibility.

If a confirmed hard disqualifier exists, stop deep research.

STAGE 2 — NEED / TIMING DISCOVERY

For plausible accounts use:

jobs
careers
news
web search
leadership changes
press releases
security/compliance pages
expansion events
relevant technology signals

STAGE 3 — DEEP RESEARCH

Use only for promising or ambiguous accounts.

Use:

additional corroboration
deeper job details
technology details
market/customer expansion
public social/company signals where appropriate

STOPPING RULES

Stop/pause research when:

hard disqualifier confirmed
Fit clearly too low
research sufficiently fresh
enough evidence exists
confidence sufficiently high
extra research unlikely to change commercial conclusion
estimated cost exceeds likely information value

Continue/deepen when:

Fit high but Need unknown
Need appears strong but Timing unclear
one strong signal needs confirmation
evidence conflicts
evidence stale
important research question unanswered
account is near an important ranking boundary

Do not use a generic LLM agent with unlimited browsing.

Use bounded research questions and deterministic provider capabilities.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

Use 100 demo companies.

Verify:

obvious non-ICP companies receive very few questions.

High-fit ambiguous companies generate relevant questions.

Questions contain provider capability.

Planner does not request every provider for every company.
