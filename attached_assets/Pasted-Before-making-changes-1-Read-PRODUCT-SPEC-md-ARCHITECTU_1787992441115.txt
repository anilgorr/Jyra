CONTENT

Preserve source text/content reference.

Do not overwrite raw content when later interpretation changes.

CONTENT HASHING

Hash normalized content.

Avoid unnecessary processing when unchanged content is fetched repeatedly.

DUPLICATION

Prevent obvious duplicate evidence by:

company
URL/content hash
observation logic

AUTHORITY / DIRECTNESS

Build simple deterministic heuristics.

Examples:

Company's own career posting:
High directness for hiring activity.

Company's official press release:
High authority for an announcement.

Unknown third-party page:
Lower authority.

Do not confuse authority with absolute truth.

GLOBAL REUSE

Company evidence describing public company facts may be globally reusable.

Tenant-specific commercial interpretation must not be written into evidence.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

Ingest several sources for one demo company.

Verify:

- source URL retained
- duplicate ingestion handled
- raw content preserved
- status transitions work
- public evidence is separate from project-specific interpretation
- evidence cannot be silently rewritten by AI
