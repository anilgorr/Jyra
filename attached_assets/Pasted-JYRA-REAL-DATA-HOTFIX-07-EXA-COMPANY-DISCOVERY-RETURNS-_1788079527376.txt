7. QUERY DESIGN RULE
==================================================

Do not put every ICP criterion into one sentence.

For example, avoid a query equivalent to:

"Find SaaS, technology, IT services, fintech, financial services,
healthcare and professional services companies in United States, India,
UAE, United Kingdom and Singapore with 100-2000 employees, Microsoft
365, Azure and significant cloud infrastructure."

That is too broad in some dimensions and too restrictive in others.

Instead test focused discovery queries such as:

QUERY A
"cloud software company with enterprise IT infrastructure"

QUERY B
"IT services company serving mid-market or enterprise customers"

QUERY C
"fintech company with significant cloud infrastructure"

Then let JYRA perform geography/size/industry qualification downstream
where data exists.

Do not require Azure/Microsoft 365 at discovery time.

Those are SOFT characteristics.
