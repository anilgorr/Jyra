==================================================
PRODUCT PRINCIPLE
==================================================

JYRA must support TWO equally important ways of building a market universe:

1. FIND MY MARKET
   JYRA discovers companies matching the customer's ICP.

2. ANALYZE MY LIST
   Customer supplies their own ICP/company/prospect dataset and JYRA determines
   which companies deserve attention now.

Customers may obtain their data from:

- their CRM
- internal databases
- CSV/XLSX files
- LinkedIn/Sales Navigator exports
- Apollo/Clay exports
- external data vendors
- conferences
- directories
- their own lawful scraping/research tools
- manually researched lists
- other legitimate sources

JYRA does NOT need to know how the customer obtained the dataset.

JYRA's job is to transform the supplied market universe into:

WHO
WHEN
WHY

==================================================
13. SOURCE DATE VS IMPORT DATE
==================================================

Do not confuse:

"When JYRA received the data"

with

"When the information was actually observed."

Example:

Customer uploads a CSV today.

Employee Count = 150

But the underlying data was collected 18 months ago.

JYRA must not treat:

150 employees

as confirmed current headcount simply because the file was uploaded today.

Track dates separately.
