7. COMMERCIAL ROLE RUBRIC
==================================================

CommercialRole must be evaluated RELATIVE TO:

Seller
+
Specific Offering
+
independently researched candidate company

Allowed roles:

POTENTIAL_BUYER
SELLER_COMPETITOR
ADJACENT_VENDOR
PARTNER_POSSIBLE
UNKNOWN

For every role persist:

role
reason
supporting evidence IDs
confidence

Use the following conceptual rubric:

SELLER_COMPETITOR

Candidate materially sells/provides substantially the same commercial
offering to similar buyers.

ADJACENT_VENDOR

Candidate sells a related/complementary product/service but does not
materially overlap the seller's core offering.

PARTNER_POSSIBLE

There is a credible complementary commercial relationship where the
candidate may distribute, refer, integrate, bundle, or cooperate with
the seller.

POTENTIAL_BUYER

Candidate is structurally capable of consuming the seller's offering
and is not primarily a competing/adjacent seller in the relationship
being evaluated.

UNKNOWN

Evidence is insufficient or commercial relationship remains genuinely
ambiguous.

Industry membership alone MUST NOT determine CommercialRole.
