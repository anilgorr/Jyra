RESEARCH NOW

Create a user-facing action:

Research Now

It must:

1. inspect current research gaps
2. generate/select research question
3. select provider
4. fetch fresh source
5. persist Evidence
6. extract Facts
7. update question status
8. update research metadata

It must NOT only reprocess existing crawl pages.

BACKGROUND REFRESH

Build a simple scheduled/background workflow suitable for Replit.

It should:

find companies due for refresh
generate/select questions
execute bounded research
save results
schedule next refresh

Until opportunity states are implemented, prioritize based on:

ICP plausibility
evidence gaps
research freshness
question importance
