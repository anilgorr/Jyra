29. BUYER ACCURACY PHILOSOPHY
==================================================

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT treat this product as requiring perfect buyer prediction.

JYRA is a MARKET PRIORITIZATION SYSTEM.

The commercial objective is:

Reduce a large possible market into a substantially better set of
accounts for sales outreach.

The long-term success metric is not:

"Can JYRA predict purchasing with 100% accuracy?"

It is:

"Does contacting JYRA-prioritized accounts produce materially better
positive replies, meetings, opportunities and wins than conventional
high-volume outbound?"

Therefore for this cycle:

Buyer precision is MEASURED.

Do NOT tune it during the blind test.

Interpretation:

< 60%
commercial-role reasoning likely still materially weak

60–70%
useful but requires improvement

70–80%
acceptable early MVP territory if false positives are safe

80–85%
strong cold-start performance

>= 85%
excellent cold-start performance

Buyer recall should also be reported.

Do NOT optimize precision by simply returning UNKNOWN for everything.

Coverage matters.
